1. Go to **Invoicing > Configuration > Settings > Taxes**. Next to *Default Taxes*, the
   *Default Service Taxes* block holds the **Sales Tax** and **Purchase Tax** used for services
   (preset to `21% S` from the Romanian chart of accounts).

   ![Settings, Taxes section: Default Taxes with 21% G and the highlighted Default Service Taxes block with 21% S](static/description/en/01_setari_taxe_servicii.png)

2. Create a product and set **Product Type** to *Service*: **Sales Taxes** and **Purchase Taxes**
   become the service taxes (`21% S`).

   ![Product form with Product Type Service and both taxes 21% S](static/description/en/02_produs_tip_serviciu.png)

3. If the type goes back to *Goods*, the service taxes are replaced by the company default taxes
   (`21% G`). Taxes set by hand are kept.

   ![Product form with Product Type Goods and both taxes 21% G](static/description/en/03_produs_revenire_bunuri.png)
