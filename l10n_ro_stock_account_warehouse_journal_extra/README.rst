Bridge between ``l10n_ro_stock_account_warehouse_journal`` and
``l10n_ro_stock_account``.

It is installed automatically when both are installed and posts in the
stock journal of the warehouse the additional Romanian entries of
``l10n_ro_stock_account`` and the FIFO negative stock compensation, as
the standard stock entry already is.
