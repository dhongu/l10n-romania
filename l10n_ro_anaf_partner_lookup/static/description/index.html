Romania - ANAF Partner Lookup

Looks a Romanian company up at ANAF by its CUI and returns the data for the partner, so that other modules can create or check a customer without depending on other localization modules.

  • CUI as typed: with or without RO, with spaces or dashes; the control digit is checked before ANAF is asked, so a mistyped code does not cost a request.
  • Partner data ready to save: name, Tax ID (with RO only for a VAT payer), street with its number, address details, locality without the Mun./Oraș/Com. prefix (a village without its commune), county, postal code, trade register number (in the NRC field) and phone. The whole address comes from one block: the registered office, the one the invoice must show, the fiscal domicile when it is missing.
  • Postal code by street: when ANAF gives none and l10n_ro_zip is installed, it is found in res.zip by street and number (with the ranges of long streets), or the one of the village.
  • Bucharest: the locality is the sector (Sector 3), as e-Factura requires; when ANAF does not write it, it is read from the postal code (030167 is in Sector 3).
  • The city from the list: where the partner has a city_id (with the cities of l10n_ro_city), it is chosen too, by name and county, the commune of a village or the postal code; without a single match it stays empty.
  • Correct diacritics: ANAF answers with the old cedilla letters (ş, ţ); they are turned into the comma ones (ș, ț).
  • Status: VAT payer, inactive and struck off, for the caller to warn about.
  • A clear answer: found, not found, invalid CUI, or ANAF unavailable — the caller can tell a company that does not exist from a service that is down. The wait is short, since somebody is usually waiting for the answer.

The module has no screen of its own; it is used by l10n_ro_pos_partner_efactura to bring a company customer in at the till.

Optional system parameters: l10n_ro_anaf_partner_lookup.url (the ANAF service) and l10n_ro_anaf_partner_lookup.timeout (seconds, 10 by default).

Changelog

19.0.1.2.2 (2026-10-29)

  • Consultant sheet (readme/FISA_CONSULTANT.md) with screenshots generated by tests/test_screenshots.py.

19.0.1.2.1 (2026-10-24)

  • The registered office comes first, the fiscal domicile only when ANAF gives no registered office: the invoice must show the registered office (Law 31/1990, art. 74). The address still comes whole from one block.

19.0.1.2.0 (2026-10-24)

  • The whole address comes from one ANAF block: the fiscal domicile, else the registered office. The postal code is no longer taken from the other block or from the general data, which gave addresses that do not exist (a street in Sector 2 with a postal code from Sector 6).
  • When the block has no postal code and l10n_ro_zip is installed, it is looked up in res.zip by street, locality and county, with the number ranges of long streets (odd and even apart); a village without streets gets the postal code of the locality. Without a single match it stays empty.

19.0.1.1.0 (2026-10-23)

  • Where the partner has a city_id (base_address_extended, with the cities of l10n_ro_city), it is filled in too: the Bucharest sector, else the locality by name in its county, told apart by the commune ANAF writes next to a village and then by the postal code, else by the postal code (street by street with l10n_ro_zip). Without a single match it is left empty.

19.0.1.0.3 (2026-10-23)

  • Main image for the Odoo Apps Store (banner); the manifest points to it in images.

19.0.1.0.2 (2026-10-23)

  • The caller can choose how long to wait for ANAF (timeout); the result also gives the date the company became inactive and the trade register number, even when the partner has no field for it.

19.0.1.0.1 (2026-10-23)

  • A village is the locality without its commune or town: “Sat Filipești Com. Filipești” gives “Filipești”; the “Orș.” prefix of a town is removed too.
  • The trade register number (J…) goes only to the NRC field: the Company ID stays the CUI, as l10n_ro computes it from the Tax ID and e-Factura uses it.

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-22)

  • Initial version: ANAF lookup by CUI (v9 service) returning the partner data, the VAT payer, inactive and struck off status, with the CUI control digit checked first and the Bucharest sector as the locality (taken from the postal code when ANAF does not write it).
  • Named l10n_ro_anaf_partner_lookup (first published as l10n_ro_anaf_lookup, renamed before any installation); the system parameters are l10n_ro_anaf_partner_lookup.url and l10n_ro_anaf_partner_lookup.timeout.
