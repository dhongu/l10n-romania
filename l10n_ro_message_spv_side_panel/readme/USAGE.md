1. Open *Invoicing > SPV > Messages SPV (split view)*.
2. Click a card: the message opens in the panel on the right and the card is highlighted. Use the
   arrows of the panel to move to the previous or the next message.
3. In the panel, read the supplier, the VAT number and the amounts, check the invoice in the tab
   *Document* and the lines in the tab *XML Lines*, then use the buttons of the panel (*Create
   Invoice*, *Accept*, *Reject* when the review is on, or download the XML, the PDF or the ZIP).
4. Use *Refresh* in the panel to read the message again after an action, and the arrow-out button
   to open the full form.
5. The *List* view keeps all the columns, the grouping and the sorting; the panel works there too.

On screens narrower than the extra-large breakpoint the panel is off and a click opens the form,
as in the original menu.
