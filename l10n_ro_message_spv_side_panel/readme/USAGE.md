1. Open *Invoicing > Accounting > SPV > Messages SPV* (with the Accounting app installed, the first
   level is called *Accounting*). The received invoices appear as a column of cards: supplier, total,
   number, date, status and the days left to the due date. A message whose archive is not downloaded
   yet is a draft: its card shows the id, the type and the date of the message.

   ![The SPV messages as a column of cards](static/description/en/01_meniu_vedere_impartita.png)

2. Click a card: the message opens in the panel on the right and the card is highlighted. The panel
   shows the supplier, the VAT number read from the XML, the dates and the amounts, and the tab
   *Document* with the invoice as written in the XML (supplier, customer, lines and totals). Use the
   arrows of the panel to move to the previous or the next message.

   ![The side panel on the tab Document](static/description/en/02_panou_document.png)

   Scroll the tab *Document* to the totals: *Total without VAT*, *VAT* and *Total* match the amounts
   of the panel.

   ![The totals of the document](static/description/en/04_document_totaluri.png)

3. Open the tab *XML Lines* to check the lines of the invoice: code, description, unit, quantity,
   unit price, VAT rate and amount.

   ![The tab XML Lines of the side panel](static/description/en/03_panou_pozitii_xml.png)

4. Use the buttons of the panel: *Create Invoice*, *Accept* and *Reject* (when the review of the
   received invoices is on), or download the XML, the PDF or the ZIP. *Refresh* reads the message
   again after an action, the arrow-out button opens the full form and the cross closes the panel.

   ![The action buttons of the panel](static/description/en/05_actiuni_panou.png)

5. The *List* view keeps all the columns, the grouping and the sorting; the panel works there too.

   ![The list with the side panel](static/description/en/06_lista_cu_panou.png)

6. The arrow-out button of the panel opens the full form: it has the VAT ID read from the XML and
   the tabs *Document* and *XML Lines* too.

   ![The full form with the XML lines](static/description/en/07_formular_complet.png)

On screens narrower than 1200 px (the extra-large breakpoint) the panel is off and a click opens the form, as
in the original menu.
