Overview

Split view for the SPV messages: the invoices received through SPV on the left, as cards, and the details of the selected one on the right, without leaving the list.

  • Cards and list
    — the menu Messages SPV opens a kanban of cards (supplier, amount, number, date, status, days to the due date; a message whose archive is not downloaded yet shows its id, type and date) and the usual list. A click selects the message and shows it in the panel.
  • The panel
    — the supplier, the VAT number read from the XML, the dates, the amounts, the review state and the buttons Create Invoice, Accept, Reject, XML, PDF and ZIP.
  • The document
    — the invoice as written in the XML, shown as a sheet of paper under the data of the message: three boxes on a row (the other party, the invoice data and our own company), the lines (code, description, unit, quantity, price, VAT rate and amount) and the totals. The panel and the full form of the message show the same document, with no tabs. Nothing is stored: the XML is read from the ZIP when the message is opened.
  • Installed on request
    — it is a bridge between l10n_ro_message_spv and deltatech_web_side_panel. The menu of the SPV messages opens the split view; uninstalling the module puts it back.

Usage

  1. Open Invoicing > Accounting > SPV > Messages SPV (with the Accounting app installed, the first level is called Accounting). The received invoices appear as a column of cards: supplier, total, number, date, status and the days left to the due date. A message whose archive is not downloaded yet is a draft: its card shows the id, the type and the date of the message.

  1. Click a card: the message opens in the panel on the right and the card is highlighted. The panel shows the partner, the VAT number read from the XML and the total, with the state of the message as a status bar next to the buttons, and the document: the invoice as written in the XML, on a sheet of paper with three boxes (the other party, the invoice data and our own company), the lines (code, description, unit, quantity, unit price, VAT rate and amount) and the totals. Use the arrows of the panel to move to the previous or the next message.

Scroll the document to the totals: Total without VAT, VAT and Total match the amounts of the panel.

  1. Use the buttons of the panel: Create Invoice, Accept and Reject (when the review of the received invoices is on), or download the XML, the PDF or the ZIP. Refresh reads the message again after an action, the arrow-out button opens the full form and the cross closes the panel.

  1. The List view keeps all the columns, the grouping and the sorting; the panel works there too.

  1. The arrow-out button of the panel opens the full form: it has the VAT ID read from the XML and the same document, with the lines. The fields are in three groups on a row (Supplier, Invoice and Amounts), the document is under them and the technical data of the message are at the bottom.

On screens narrower than 1200 px (the extra-large breakpoint) the panel is off and a click opens the form of the message.

Versions

19.0.1.3.0 (2026-11-13)

  • The state of the message is a status bar next to the buttons, in the panel and in the full form.
  • The panel no longer repeats what the document shows (reference, dates, currency, untaxed amount, tax and the title): it keeps the partner, the VAT number read from the XML, the invoice, the total (red when it differs from the invoice) and the review state.
  • The boxes of the document follow the direction of the invoice: the other party on the left, the invoice in the middle and our own company on the right, in a lighter color (the supplier on an issued invoice, the customer on a received one).
  • The sheet of paper has only a soft shadow, without a border and without the second "page" behind.

19.0.1.2.0 (2026-11-13)

  • The tabs Document and XML Lines are gone: the document (the invoice as written in the XML, with its lines and totals) is shown directly under the data of the message, in the panel and in the full form. The lines were shown twice, in both tabs.
  • The document looks like a sheet of paper (border and shadow), to show that it is the invoice as received, and the supplier, the customer and the data of the invoice are three boxes on a row (fewer on a narrow panel).
  • The fields of the full form are regrouped in three groups on a row (Supplier, Invoice and Amounts), followed by the document; the technical data of the message (message id, type, request id, VAT number, date, details) are in the group Message at the bottom.

19.0.1.1.0 (2026-11-13)

  • One entry in the menu: Messages SPV (and its parent SPV) opens the split view; the extra entry Messages SPV (split view) is gone. The uninstall hook puts the menus back on the original action.
  • The full form of the message has the VAT ID read from the XML and the tabs Document and XML Lines, like the panel.
  • The lines show the code of the item from the XML: the code of the seller, or the standard identifier (e.g. GTIN) when there is none.
  • A draft (a message whose archive is not downloaded yet) is a useful card: it shows the id, the type and the date of the message and says that the archive has not been downloaded, instead of an empty card with 0.00.
  • The document and the lines built from the XML use the size of the text of the form, smaller than before, in the panel and in the full form.

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-11-13)

  • First release: the menu Messages SPV (split view) with a kanban and a list that show the selected message in a side panel (deltatech_web_side_panel). The panel reads the supplier VAT number, the document and the lines from the invoice XML when the message is opened.
