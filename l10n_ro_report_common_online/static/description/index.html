Overview

The common building blocks of the Romanian printed reports, for Odoo Online too: the module has no Python code and is imported as a zip (Apps > Import Module). It is the data-only variant of l10n_ro_report_common, for databases where custom Python cannot run.

  • l10n_ro_report_common_online.banks
    prints up to 3 of the partner's bank accounts ticked Romania - Print in Report, in the currency of the document (or of the company).
  • l10n_ro_report_common_online.report_address_company
    the company identification block: name, address, bank accounts, Tax ID, NRC and share capital.
  • l10n_ro_report_common_online.amount_in_words
    the amount in words the way a Romanian receipt reads it, written in QWeb. Call it with amount and currency:
amount text
500 cinci sute de lei
12000 douăsprezece mii de lei
21000 douăzeci și una de mii de lei
1.01 un leu și un ban
-500.25 minus cinci sute de lei și douăzeci și cinci de bani

RON is spelled out in Romanian whatever the print language; any other currency keeps the standard Odoo wording.

The module adds two fields, both manual (x_), as Odoo Online requires:

  • Romania - Print in Report (x_l10n_ro_print_report) on bank accounts;
  • Share Capital (x_l10n_ro_share_capital) on the company.

Odoo Online

The module contains no Python: it can be imported on Odoo Online (Custom plan) from Apps > Import Module. Do not install it next to l10n_ro_report_common: the two modules keep the same data in different fields.

Usage

Activate the Romanian language before importing the module (Apps > Import Module, in developer mode), so that its labels are translated. The module needs l10n_ro; the NRC printed in the header is the NRC field of the company contact.

  1. Open Settings > Users & Companies > Companies, open the company and fill in Share Capital under Company ID. It is printed without decimals.

  1. Open Contacts > Configuration > Bank Accounts > Bank Accounts, open the company account and tick Romania - Print in Report. Only the accounts in the currency of the document are printed, at most 3.

  1. The Romania - Print in Report column of the bank account list shows which accounts are printed.

  1. The module has no report of its own: the result shows on the reports that call its templates (report_address_company, banks, amount_in_words). Below, a test rendering of the company block and of the amount in words.
