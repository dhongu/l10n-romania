Romania - Invoice Report for Odoo Online

The Romanian layout of the invoice and of the cash receipt (chitanță), for Odoo Online too: the module has no Python code and is imported as a zip (Apps > Import Module). It is the data-only variant of l10n_ro_invoice_report, for databases where custom Python cannot run. It needs l10n_ro_report_common_online, imported first.

Invoice (the standard Invoices report, changed in place):

  • header with the supplier and the customer side by side: address, bank accounts, CIF, NRC, share capital, VAT on payment, delivery address; the operating unit of the journal, when set;
  • lines: optional line number, unit price without VAT, VAT value per line, optional total with VAT, optional list price before discount, the product name removed from the description on request; credit notes printed with negative values;
  • footer: issuer, delegate and means of transport, the deliveries (or receptions) of the invoice with their AWB numbers, the legal mention that the invoice is valid without signature and stamp, the additional info of the customer, the optional Coface logo;
  • invoices paid in cash carry the cash receipt below, with the amount in words.

Other reports: Voucher / Payment (receipt or payment disposal, Form 14-4-1 / 14-4-4 for the cash desk) and Invoices in company language.

Settings are on the company, Romanian Invoice tab: e-mail, phone and marker in the addresses, line number, total with VAT, list price before discount, product name, payment communication, deliveries, delegate, Coface logo.

Automations (instead of Python): the delegate brings its means of transport on the invoice; the partner’s Payment bank account is set on its customer invoices; the action Set origin from pickings writes the deliveries in the origin of the invoice.

What it does not do, compared with l10n_ro_invoice_report: no changes to the bank statements and no default delegate from the context.

Odoo Online

The module contains no Python: it can be imported on Odoo Online (Custom plan) from Apps > Import Module. It cannot be installed next to l10n_ro_invoice_report (the manifest excludes it): the two modules keep the same data in different fields.

Changelog

19.0.1.0.0

  • First version: the data-only variant of l10n_ro_invoice_report, importable on Odoo Online. The report templates are the same QWeb, on manual x_ fields; the helpers of the report (deliveries, receipt detection, journal type and direction of the payments, amount in words) are computed in QWeb; the onchange of the delegate, the payment bank account of the partner and Set origin from pickings are automation rules and server actions; the settings are on the company form. The manifest excludes l10n_ro_invoice_report.
