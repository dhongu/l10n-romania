Overview

The Romanian layout of the invoice and of the cash receipt (chitanță), for Odoo Online too: the module has no Python code and is imported as a zip (Apps > Import Module). It is the data-only variant of l10n_ro_invoice_report, for databases where custom Python cannot run. It needs l10n_ro_report_common_online, imported first.

Invoice (the standard Invoices report, changed in place):

  • ✓
    header with the supplier and the customer side by side: address, bank accounts, CIF, NRC, share capital, VAT on payment, delivery address; the operating unit of the journal, when set;
  • ✓
    lines: optional line number, unit price without VAT, VAT value per line, optional total with VAT, optional list price before discount, the product name removed from the description on request; credit notes printed with negative values;
  • ✓
    footer: issuer, delegate and means of transport, the deliveries (or receptions) of the invoice with their AWB numbers, the legal mention that the invoice is valid without signature and stamp, the additional info of the customer, the optional Coface logo;
  • ✓
    invoices paid in cash carry the cash receipt below, with the amount in words.

Other reports: Voucher / Payment (receipt or payment disposal, Form 14-4-1 / 14-4-4 for the cash desk) and Invoices in company language.

Settings are on the company, Romanian Invoice tab: e-mail, phone and marker in the addresses, line number, total with VAT, list price before discount, product name, payment communication, deliveries, delegate, Coface logo.

Automations (instead of Python): the delegate brings its means of transport on the invoice; the partner's Payment bank account is set on its customer invoices; the action Set origin from pickings writes the deliveries in the origin of the invoice.

What it does not do, compared with l10n_ro_invoice_report: no changes to the bank statements and no default delegate from the context.

Odoo Online

The module contains no Python: it can be imported on Odoo Online (Custom plan) from Apps > Import Module. It cannot be installed next to l10n_ro_invoice_report (the manifest excludes it): the two modules keep the same data in different fields.

Usage

Import l10n_ro_report_common_online first, then this module, from Apps > Import Module.

1. Invoice options, on the company. In Settings > Users & Companies > Companies, open the company and go to the Romanian Invoice tab. Tick what the invoice prints: the address details (marker, e-mail, phone), the lines (index line, total with taxes, product name removed, undiscounted price) and the footer (payment communication, pickings, delegate, Coface logo).

On the company bank account, tick Romania - Print in Report: the account is printed on the invoices in its currency (or in the company currency, when the account has none).

2. Operating unit, on the sales journal. In Invoicing > Configuration > Journals, set Operating Unit on the sales journal. Its name and address are printed under the supplier; its bank accounts, when it has any, replace those of the company.

3. Partner fields. On the partner, Sales & Purchase tab, Misc group: Payment bank account (the company account the customer pays into, set on its draft customer invoices) and Mean transport (filled on the delegate, copied on the invoice).

On the Invoicing tab, Additional info for invoice is printed at the end of the partner's invoices.

If you use the payment bank account, mark the company bank account as trusted (Send Money), otherwise Odoo does not confirm the invoices on which the account was set.

4. Delegate on the invoice. On a draft customer invoice, choose the Delegate (a person, not a company): the Mean transport of the delegate is copied on the invoice.

5. Origin from pickings. On an invoice from a sale or purchase order, Actions > Set origin from pickings writes the done deliveries (or receptions) in the Source of the invoice.

6. Printed invoice. Print > Invoice PDF prints the supplier and the customer side by side, the operating unit, the lines with the VAT value per line, the issuer, the delegate, the means of transport, the deliveries with their AWB, the legal mention, the additional info of the customer and the optional Coface logo. Invoices in company language prints it in the language of the company partner.

An invoice paid on a cash journal carries the cash receipt below, with the amount in words, on Invoice PDF only (not on PDF without Payment).

7. Voucher / Payment. From a payment, Actions > Print > Voucher / Payment prints the receipt (inbound) or the payment disposal (outbound). On a cash journal it adds the form code (14-4-1 or 14-4-4), the cash desk and, for payments, the identity document line.

8. Due filters. On the customer invoices, the filters Due tomorrow, Due today, Overdue 1-7 days, Overdue 8-14 days, Overdue 15-30 days and Overdue more than 30 days list the posted invoices not fully paid.
