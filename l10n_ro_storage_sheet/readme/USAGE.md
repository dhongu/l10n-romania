1. Go to **Inventory > Reporting > Romanian Stock Sheet Report**, choose the **Location** and the
   **Date** range (the current month by default), then click **Show Sheet**.

   ![Stock sheet wizard with the location, the date range and the Show Sheet button highlighted](static/description/en/01_wizard_fisa_magazie.png)

2. The sheet opens as a pivot. Group the rows by **Product**: each product shows the initial,
   input, output and final quantity and amount. Export it with the download (XLSX) button.

   ![Stock sheet pivot grouped by product, with initial, input, output and final quantities and amounts](static/description/en/02_pivot_fisa_magazie.png)

3. Switch to the **list** view to see each move with its document; the **INITIAL** and **FINAL**
   rows of each product are the opening and closing balances of the period.

   ![Stock sheet list with one row per move with the INITIAL and FINAL rows per product](static/description/en/03_lista_miscari.png)
