Overview

This module adds the Romanian storage sheet (Fișă Magazie) report: opening balance, receipts, deliveries and closing balance per product and location, as a PDF or as a pivot analysis.

It works on the native Odoo stock valuation and does not depend on the l10n_ro_stock_account valuation engine.

It was previously published in this repository as l10n_ro_stock_report, which clashed with the OCA module of the same name. Databases that need the valued move types of the OCA engine keep using the OCA l10n_ro_stock_report.

Configuration

This module is the successor of the Terrabit copy of l10n_ro_stock_report. It is not installed automatically in its place: the old name now resolves to the OCA l10n_ro_stock_report module, which depends on l10n_ro_stock_account.

On a database where the storage sheet was installed without l10n_ro_stock_account, install l10n_ro_storage_sheet and uninstall l10n_ro_stock_report before updating, otherwise the update also installs the OCA stock valuation engine.

The report models are transient, so no data is migrated.

Usage

  1. Go to Inventory > Reporting > Romanian Stock Sheet Report, choose the Location and the Date range (the current month by default), then click Show Sheet.

  1. The sheet opens as a pivot. Group the rows by Product: each product shows the initial, input, output and final quantity and amount. Export it with the download (XLSX) button.

  1. Switch to the list view to see each move with its document; the INITIAL and FINAL rows of each product are the opening and closing balances of the period.

  1. Back in the wizard, Show Sheet Pdf prints the storage sheet: per product and stock account, the INITIAL row, each move and the FINAL row, with the stock after every operation.
