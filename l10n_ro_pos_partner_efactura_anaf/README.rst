The ANAF data of a company is often incomplete or outdated: a street
without a number, a city that is not in the declared county, details of
the address moved into the street. e-Factura (SPV) refuses an invoice
with such an address, and by then the customer has left the shop.

This module joins *Romania - POS Partner e-Factura Check* and *Romania -
Point of Sale Partner* (OCA), and is installed automatically when both
are present. A customer fetched with **Create from ANAF** in the Point
of Sale is no longer put on the order directly:

- the partner form opens with the data from ANAF and a warning listing
  what looks wrong: missing data, a street without a number, a city that
  is not in the county;
- the cashier checks the data against the customer's documents, corrects
  it and saves; the form cannot be saved while the data e-Factura needs
  is missing;
- only then the customer is put on the order. Closing the form without
  saving leaves the customer off the order, still waiting to be
  confirmed: picking it later from the customer list opens the form
  again.

A customer already on file, found by its Tax ID, goes on the order
directly, unless its data is incomplete; then it is confirmed the same
way.
