Looks a Romanian company up at ANAF by its CUI and returns the data for
the partner, so that other modules can create or check a customer
without depending on other localization modules.

- **CUI as typed**: with or without ``RO``, with spaces or dashes; the
  control digit is checked before ANAF is asked, so a mistyped code does
  not cost a request.
- **Partner data ready to save**: name, Tax ID (with ``RO`` only for a
  VAT payer), street with its number, address details, locality without
  the ``Mun.``/``Oraș``/``Com.`` prefix, county, postal code, trade
  register number and phone. The fiscal domicile is used, the registered
  office when it is missing.
- **Bucharest**: the locality is the sector (``Sector 3``), as e-Factura
  requires.
- **Correct diacritics**: ANAF answers with the old cedilla letters
  (``ş``, ``ţ``); they are turned into the comma ones (``ș``, ``ț``).
- **Status**: VAT payer, inactive and struck off, for the caller to warn
  about.
- **A clear answer**: found, not found, invalid CUI, or ANAF unavailable
  — the caller can tell a company that does not exist from a service
  that is down. The wait is short, since somebody is usually waiting for
  the answer.

The module has no screen of its own; it is used by
`l10n_ro_pos_partner_efactura <../l10n_ro_pos_partner_efactura>`__ to
bring a company customer in at the till.

Optional system parameters: ``l10n_ro_anaf_lookup.url`` (the ANAF
service) and ``l10n_ro_anaf_lookup.timeout`` (seconds, 10 by default).
