1. Receive the goods in the store location, or transfer them from a depot. Next
   to the stock entry at cost, a journal entry 371 = 378 / 4428 is posted.
2. Open the stock move (*Inventory > Reporting > Moves History*): the **Store
   Valuation** group shows the sale amount, the markup, the uneligible VAT and the
   journal entry. The same columns can be shown in the list view.
3. When the goods leave the store, the entry 378 / 4428 = 371 releases the markup
   and the VAT at the sale price of the entry.
