Overview

The standard partner autocomplete of Odoo suggests companies from IAP and spends credits to fill the partner in. For a Romanian company the authoritative source is ANAF, and it is free.

With this module, typing a Romanian CUI where Odoo offers the autocomplete — the Tax ID or the Name of a partner, the Customer of a quotation or an invoice, the customer form opened from the Point of Sale — shows the company found at ANAF. Choosing it fills the partner in:

  • ✓
    name, Tax ID (with RO only for a VAT payer), VAT payer flag, street with its number, address details, locality, county, postal code, trade register number and phone;
  • ✓
    for Bucharest, the sector as the locality, as e-Factura requires;
  • ✓
    a note in the chatter that the data was taken from ANAF, with the date.

Companies from other EU countries, from VIES. A VAT number with the prefix of another EU country (IT00743110157, HU10773381) is looked up in VIES, the free service of the European Commission: the name, street, postal code, city and country are filled in, with a note in the chatter, and no IAP credit is used. When VIES does not answer, does not know the number or the country keeps the name to itself (Germany, Spain), IAP is asked as before. The update button next to the Tax ID of such a company reloads the data from VIES and sets Intra-Community Valid.

VIES for a Romanian company. For a VAT payer, the note after the ANAF data says whether the company is in VIES, that is registered for intra-community operations; a VAT payer is not always.

Update from ANAF. On a Romanian company with a Tax ID, the Update from ANAF button (the refresh icon) next to the Tax ID reloads the data from ANAF: name, address, Tax ID and trade register number are rewritten, a phone already on the partner is kept. The chatter lists every field changed, with its old and new value, and says when ANAF lists the company as struck off or inactive. With l10n_ro_anaf_partner, the VAT on Payment (RTVAI) flag is filled in and updated the same way.

A partner created with its CUI as the name. Typing a CUI in a field such as Customer and choosing Create, or importing partners whose name is a CUI, fills them in from ANAF too. Values given explicitly are kept; a partner that already has a Tax ID, a contact person or a partner from another country is left as it is. The l10n_ro_anaf_no_autofill context key turns it off, for instance for a bulk import.

VAT payer. A Romanian company shows VAT Payer on its form, filled in from ANAF together with the rest of the data. The partner also keeps its Tax ID without the country prefix. Both fields have the names used by the other Romanian localization modules, so no other module is needed for them.

City and postal code. With the list of Romanian cities (l10n_ro_city), the city is chosen from it as well. Choosing a city then keeps the postal code the partner already has, instead of replacing it with the city's.

No IAP credit is used for a Romanian CUI: neither the search nor the enrichment goes to IAP.

The CUI is recognised with or without RO. The rest is left to IAP as before: a search by company name, a company from outside the EU, a company from the EU that VIES does not describe, and a Romanian CUI when ANAF does not answer.

The Enrich action of the partner list and form (Odoo 20) asks ANAF as well for a Romanian company, by its Tax ID or its Company ID, and VIES for a company from another EU country, and fills only the fields the partner does not have yet.

Intra-Community Valid comes with the standard l10n_eu_account_vies module (installed by Verify VAT Numbers in the accounting settings); without it, the VIES answer is only written in the chatter.

Versions

20.0.1.5.0 (2026-11-04)

  • A VAT number of another EU country is answered from VIES, without IAP credits: name, street, postal code, city and country. IAP is asked as before when VIES does not answer, does not know the number, or the member state keeps the name to itself (Germany, Spain).
  • Update from VIES on a company from another EU country rewrites the name and the address VIES gives, keeps what it does not give, and sets Intra-Community Valid.
  • For a Romanian VAT payer the note after the ANAF data, and the one after Update from ANAF, say whether the company is in VIES, that is registered for intra-community operations.
  • The update buttons show only their icon, in the row of the Tax ID instead of a whole row below it; the name is in the tooltip.
  • The Enrich action fills a partner with a VAT number of another EU country from VIES too, without IAP credits, when VIES gives the name and the address.
  • Intra-Community Valid is the field of l10n_eu_account_vies in 20.0 (base_vat in 19.0): it is set only when that module is installed (Verify VAT Numbers in the accounting settings).
  • The Apps banner and the summary of the manifest name VIES next to ANAF.
  • Update from VIES is also shown for a United Kingdom partner with an XI number (Northern Ireland, which stays in VIES for goods); a GB number is not in VIES.
  • Update from VIES clears the old street details next to a new street and the old state next to a new city, as Update from ANAF does.
  • With l10n_ro_config (OCA) installed, the partner form showed VAT Payer twice: the box this module puts with the Tax ID and the one l10n_ro_config adds under the trade register number. The l10n_ro_config one is dropped from the form.

20.0.1.4.1 (2026-11-03)

  • Update from ANAF no longer clears the street, the street details or the postal code when ANAF has none for the company and the city and county are the partner's: the street is required on an e-Factura invoice (CIUS-RO). When ANAF gives another locality they are still cleared, so the old street does not stay on the new city; next to a new street from ANAF the old street details and postal code are cleared too.

20.0.1.4.0 (2026-11-03)

  • The partner has the Romanian VAT fields of its own, so it no longer needs l10n_ro_config for them: VAT Payer (l10n_ro_vat_subjected), shown on the form of a Romanian company and filled in from ANAF, and l10n_ro_vat_number, the Tax ID without the country prefix. The names and types are those of l10n_ro_config and l10n_ro_anaf_partner, so the data already there is kept.
  • The chatter note of Update from ANAF shows a check box as Yes / No, not as True / False.

20.0.1.3.1 (2026-10-29)

  • The Update from ANAF link takes a whole row, so the identifiers keep their labels; the chatter note of an ANAF suggestion shows the date in the user's language format.
  • The settings note says that the Tax ID field needs the RO prefix for the suggestions. Consultant sheet with screenshots.

20.0.1.3.0 (2026-10-28)

  • Migration to 20.0. is_company is computed from the Tax ID and the CUI is the Company ID (RO_EN) deduced from it: the ANAF data no longer carries either.
  • The Update from ANAF link sits below the Tax ID row, next to which 20.0 shows the identifiers.
  • The Enrich action (new in 20.0) answers a Romanian CUI from ANAF, without IAP credit.

19.0.1.3.0 (2026-10-27)

  • It cannot be installed together with the OCA l10n_ro_partner_create_by_vat (excludes): both fill the partner from ANAF, each its own way. Uninstall it first, with the modules that depend on it (l10n_ro_partner_create_by_vat_button, l10n_ro_fiscal_validation).

19.0.1.2.0 (2026-10-23)

  • The ANAF suggestion carries the city_id found by l10n_ro_anaf_partner_lookup.
  • For a Romanian partner, choosing the city keeps the postal code already there (base_address_extended would replace it with the city's, often empty for a town), and a city set together with its postal code is not replaced from that postal code (l10n_ro_city picks the Bucharest sector from it, and ANAF sometimes gives a postal code of another sector).

19.0.1.1.3 (2026-10-23)

  • Main image for the Odoo Apps Store (banner); the manifest points to it in images.

19.0.1.1.2 (2026-10-23)

  • A note under Partner Autocomplete in the settings says that Romanian companies are filled in from ANAF without IAP credits, and what the credits are still used for.

19.0.1.1.1 (2026-10-23)

  • A partner created with a CUI as its name is filled in from ANAF also when its Tax ID is that same CUI, as l10n_ro_partner_create_by_vat_button sets it before this module runs; a Tax ID of another company still leaves the partner as it is.
