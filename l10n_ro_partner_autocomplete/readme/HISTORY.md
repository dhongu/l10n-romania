## 19.0.1.5.1 (2026-11-05)

- The description and the consultant sheet explain what *Intra-Community Valid* means for VAT: a
  valid VIES number makes the customer B2B (exempt intra-community supply), without it the customer
  is B2C (Romanian VAT below the EUR 10,000 threshold, the VAT of its country through OSS above it),
  and how Odoo picks the fiscal position from the box.

## 19.0.1.5.0 (2026-11-04)

- A VAT number of another EU country is answered from VIES, without IAP credits: name, street,
  postal code, city and country. IAP is asked as before when VIES does not answer, does not know
  the number, or the member state keeps the name to itself (Germany, Spain).
- **Update from VIES** on a company from another EU country rewrites the name and the address VIES
  gives, keeps what it does not give, and sets *Intra-Community Valid*.
- For a Romanian VAT payer the note after the ANAF data, and the one after **Update from ANAF**,
  say whether the company is in VIES, that is registered for intra-community operations.
- The update buttons under the Tax ID show only their icon; the name is in the tooltip.
- The Apps banner and the summary of the manifest name VIES next to ANAF.
- **Update from VIES** is also shown for a United Kingdom partner with an `XI` number (Northern
  Ireland, which stays in VIES for goods); a `GB` number is not in VIES.
- **Update from VIES** clears the old street details next to a new street and the old state next
  to a new city, as **Update from ANAF** does.
- With `l10n_ro_config` (OCA) installed, the partner form showed **VAT Payer** twice: the box this
  module puts with the Tax ID and the one `l10n_ro_config` adds under the trade register number.
  The `l10n_ro_config` one is dropped from the form.

## 19.0.1.4.1 (2026-11-03)

- **Update from ANAF** no longer clears the street, the street details or the postal code
  when ANAF has none for the company and the city and county are the partner's: the street is
  required on an e-Factura invoice (CIUS-RO). When ANAF gives another locality they are still
  cleared, so the old street does not stay on the new city; next to a new street from ANAF the
  old street details and postal code are cleared too.

## 19.0.1.4.0 (2026-11-03)

- The partner has the Romanian VAT fields of its own, so it no longer needs `l10n_ro_config`
  for them: **VAT Payer** (`l10n_ro_vat_subjected`), shown with the Tax ID of a Romanian
  company and filled in from ANAF, and `l10n_ro_vat_number`, the Tax ID without the country
  prefix. The names and types are those of `l10n_ro_config` and `l10n_ro_anaf_partner`, so the
  data already there is kept.
- The chatter note of **Update from ANAF** shows a check box as *Yes* / *No*, not as
  *True* / *False*.
