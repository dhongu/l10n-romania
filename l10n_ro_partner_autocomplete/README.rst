The standard partner autocomplete of Odoo suggests companies from IAP
and spends credits to fill the partner in. For a Romanian company the
authoritative source is ANAF, and it is free.

With this module, typing a Romanian CUI where Odoo offers the
autocomplete — the *Tax ID* or the *Name* of a partner, the *Customer*
of a quotation or an invoice, the customer form opened from the Point of
Sale — shows the company found at ANAF. Choosing it fills the partner
in:

- name, Tax ID (with ``RO`` only for a VAT payer), street with its
  number, address details, locality, county, postal code, trade register
  number and phone;
- for Bucharest, the sector as the locality, as e-Factura requires;
- a note in the chatter that the data was taken from ANAF, with the
  date.

**Update from ANAF.** On a Romanian company with a Tax ID, the **Update
from ANAF** button below the Tax ID reloads the data from ANAF: name,
address, Tax ID and trade register number are rewritten, a phone already
on the partner is kept. The chatter lists every field changed, with its
old and new value, and says when ANAF lists the company as struck off or
inactive.

**A partner created with its CUI as the name.** Typing a CUI in a field
such as *Customer* and choosing *Create*, or importing partners whose
name is a CUI, fills them in from ANAF too. Values given explicitly are
kept; a partner that already has a Tax ID, a contact person or a partner
from another country is left as it is. The ``l10n_ro_anaf_no_autofill``
context key turns it off, for instance for a bulk import.

**City and postal code.** With the list of Romanian cities
(``l10n_ro_city``), the city is chosen from it as well. Choosing a city
then keeps the postal code the partner already has, instead of replacing
it with the city's.

No IAP credit is used for a Romanian CUI: neither the search nor the
enrichment goes to IAP.

The CUI is recognised with or without ``RO``. The rest is left to IAP as
before: a search by company name, a company from another country, and a
Romanian CUI when ANAF does not answer.

Changelog
=========

19.0.1.3.0 (2026-10-27)
-----------------------

- It cannot be installed together with the OCA
  ``l10n_ro_partner_create_by_vat`` (``excludes``): both fill the
  partner from ANAF, each its own way. Uninstall it first, with the
  modules that depend on it (``l10n_ro_partner_create_by_vat_button``,
  ``l10n_ro_fiscal_validation``).

19.0.1.2.0 (2026-10-23)
-----------------------

- The ANAF suggestion carries the ``city_id`` found by
  ``l10n_ro_anaf_partner_lookup``.
- For a Romanian partner, choosing the city keeps the postal code
  already there (base_address_extended would replace it with the city's,
  often empty for a town), and a city set together with its postal code
  is not replaced from that postal code (``l10n_ro_city`` picks the
  Bucharest sector from it, and ANAF sometimes gives a postal code of
  another sector).

19.0.1.1.3 (2026-10-23)
-----------------------

- Main image for the Odoo Apps Store (banner); the manifest points to it
  in ``images``.

19.0.1.1.2 (2026-10-23)
-----------------------

- A note under *Partner Autocomplete* in the settings says that Romanian
  companies are filled in from ANAF without IAP credits, and what the
  credits are still used for.

19.0.1.1.1 (2026-10-23)
-----------------------

- A partner created with a CUI as its name is filled in from ANAF also
  when its Tax ID is that same CUI, as
  ``l10n_ro_partner_create_by_vat_button`` sets it before this module
  runs; a Tax ID of another company still leaves the partner as it is.

19.0.1.1.0 (2026-10-23)
-----------------------

- **Update from ANAF** button on a Romanian company: the data is
  reloaded from ANAF, a phone already set is kept, and the chatter lists
  the changed fields with their old and new value.
- A partner created with a Romanian CUI as its name (quick create,
  import, API) is filled in from ANAF; the ``l10n_ro_anaf_no_autofill``
  context key turns it off.

19.0.1.0.1 (2026-10-22)
-----------------------

- Depends on ``l10n_ro_anaf_partner_lookup``, the new name of
  ``l10n_ro_anaf_lookup``.

19.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-22)
-----------------------

- Initial version: the partner autocomplete answers a Romanian CUI (with
  or without ``RO``) from ANAF instead of IAP, search and enrichment,
  without IAP credits; IAP keeps the name search, the other countries
  and the case when ANAF does not answer.
