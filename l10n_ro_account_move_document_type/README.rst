Adds the **supporting document type** (``l10n_ro_document_type``) to
journal entries, so that the Romanian journals and registers can show
which document justifies each entry: invoice, credit note, vendor bill,
customs import declaration (DVI), cash receipt, cash disposal, bank
statement, payroll, expense report or a plain journal entry.

- Invoices and receipts take the type from the kind of document.
- Payments and bank statement lines take it from the journal: *Bank
  Statement* on bank journals, *Cash Document* on cash journals.
- Every other entry is a *Journal Entry* until the module that creates
  it says otherwise (the DVI entry of ``l10n_ro_customs_dvi``, the cash
  receipts and disposals of ``l10n_ro_account_sequence``).
- On a journal entry the accountant can change the type from the form,
  for example an import VAT entry recorded by hand, whose supporting
  document is the DVI.

The VAT purchase and sales journal of ``l10n_ro_account_vat_journal``
uses it for the *Document Type* column.

Usage
=====

1. Open a journal entry in *Accounting > Accounting > Journal Entries*.
2. Next to the *Reference*, choose the *Document Type* when the entry is
   justified by another document than a journal entry (for example
   *Import (DVI)* for the import VAT recorded by hand).
3. In the list of journal entries, show the *Document Type* column from
   the optional columns or group by *Document Type*.

Invoices, payments and the entries created from a DVI or from a cash
receipt get the type automatically.
