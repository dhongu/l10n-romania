1. Open a journal entry in *Accounting > Accounting > Journal Entries*.
2. Next to the *Reference*, choose the *Document Type* when the entry is justified by another
   document than a journal entry (for example *Import (DVI)* for the import VAT recorded by hand).
3. In the list of journal entries, show the *Document Type* column from the optional columns or
   group by *Document Type*.

Invoices, payments and the entries created from a DVI or from a cash receipt get the type
automatically.
