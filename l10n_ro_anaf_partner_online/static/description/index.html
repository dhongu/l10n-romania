Romania - ANAF Partner Data for Odoo Online

Fill in Romanian companies from ANAF by their CUI (Tax ID), on Odoo Online too: the module has no Python code and is imported as a zip (Apps > Import Module). The lookup itself runs on the Terrabit service, which asks ANAF and sends the data back to your database.

  • ANAF button on the partner form: next to the Tax ID, it fills in the name, the address of the registered office (county, city, postal code), the Tax ID with RO only for VAT payers and the phone, before you save.
  • Many partners at once: Actions > Update from ANAF on a selection of partners; the service answers in a few seconds and posts a note in the chatter of each partner found.
  • ANAF status on the partner: VAT payer, fiscally inactive, struck off, the trade register number (J…) and the date of the last check, on the ANAF tab.
  • Activation in one click: Contacts > Configuration > ANAF > ANAF Service, Activate; the service token is written in your database automatically, with free trial lookups. Buy Credits and Refresh Credits are on the same screen (module IAP Services for Odoo Online, deltatech_iap_online, installed with this one).
  • Who sees what: the users who edit partners (Contact Creation) can read the service account, because the ANAF button asks the service from their browser; the button and Update from ANAF are for them. Only administrators activate the service, buy credits and read the requests and their answers (access rights of deltatech_iap_online).
  • What leaves your database: the CUI you look up, the account and request tokens, the id of the request, the UUID and the address of your database (for the answer); at activation, also the name and the Tax ID of the company. The requests, their status and errors stay visible under ANAF Requests.

The service is provided by Terrabit: after the trial lookups, more can be bought from Terrabit.

Important

This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning. Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production. More details on development status

Usage

Activate the service (administrator, once)

  1. Go to Contacts > Configuration: the ANAF group holds ANAF Service and ANAF Requests (administrators only).

  2. Open ANAF Service and click Activate. The account token comes back by itself in a few seconds, with free trial lookups. Once active, the header shows Activate Again, Refresh Credits and Buy Credits, and Credits Left shows the lookups left on the Terrabit account (updated by Refresh Credits and by every queued answer). One credit is used per ANAF answer (company found or not found); an invalid CUI or ANAF not answering is free.

Fill in a new partner

  1. Create a contact, choose Company and type the CUI in VAT (with or without RO). Press Tab so that the suggestion list of the field closes, then click ANAF next to it. The button is hidden on the contacts of a company.

  2. The form is filled in at once, before you save: name, VAT with RO only for VAT payers, the address of the registered office (in Bucharest, the city is the sector) and the phone, if ANAF has it. A notification shows the lookups left. Check the data, then save. If the browser cannot reach the service, the partner is saved and the lookup is queued instead.

  3. After saving, the ANAF tab shows VAT Payer (ANAF), Fiscally Inactive (ANAF), Struck Off (ANAF), Trade Register No. and Last ANAF Check. The tab appears once the partner has been checked at ANAF.

Update many partners

  1. In the list of contacts, select the partners and click Actions > Update from ANAF (also in the Actions menu of the partner form). Partners without a VAT number are skipped.

  2. A notification tells how many requests were sent; the partners are updated in a few seconds.

  3. Each partner found gets the ANAF data and the note Partner data updated from ANAF (Terrabit service). in the chatter, with the tracked changes. The answer is written only if the user who sent the request may edit the partner and the partner still has the same CUI.

Follow the requests (administrator)

  1. Contacts > Configuration > ANAF > ANAF Requests lists every request with its Request Type, Name (the CUI looked up), Status and Error; the requests in error are red (for example Invalid CUI). A request still without answer after 10 minutes is sent once more.
